# FORGE — Your Training. Your Progress. Your Records.

> The Forge Athlete App gives you everything you need to track your workouts, measure your progress, and see how far you've come — all from your phone.

---

## What Is the Forge Athlete App?

The Forge Athlete App is the training companion for members of The Forge Functional Fitness. It connects directly to your coach's programming and — uniquely — **your coach logs your results for you**. No more racing to write your score on a whiteboard photo before you forget. No more "what did I get last time?" Just walk out, open the app, and everything is already there.

That's the headline difference between Forge and other gym apps: you're not the data-entry clerk. Your coach enters scores for the whole class during or after the workout, and your training history builds itself.

---

## What You Get

### See Your Workouts

- **Today's workout, ready and waiting** — as soon as your coach publishes the WOD, it appears in your app, formatted exactly as it's written on the whiteboard
- **Filter by class type** — only see workouts relevant to you (WOD, Foundations, Kids & Teens)
- **Coach notes** — read the intended stimulus and scaling guidance before you even walk through the door

### Your Results — Done For You

- **No score entry on your part** — your coach enters scores for the whole class in the Score Entry tool. By the time you've stretched and showered, your result is already in the app
- **Section-by-section** — every part of the workout (Strength, WOD, Skills, Accessory) is logged separately so nothing gets lost
- **Scaling and track tagged correctly** — your coach assigns the scaling level and track you actually did (up to 3 scaling levels per section, Tracks 1/2/3 where applicable) so the leaderboard ranks you against the right peer group
- **DNF support** — didn't finish in the time cap? Your coach marks DNF. Your partial effort still appears on the leaderboard but ranks below athletes who completed
- **Coach-defined scoring** — if your coach sets up custom metrics for a workout (e.g. RPE, partition splits), they appear in your log automatically
- **Browse your history** — view your logbook by day, week, or month. Scroll back through weeks and months of training to see your consistency
- **Whiteboard photos** — if you want a personal memory of the day, snap a quick photo of the whiteboard and attach it to your log entry. Purely optional extra context — the scores themselves are already in

### What You Can Still Log Yourself

The coach drives the class-day logbook. But for anything you do **outside class**, you've got full control:

- **Benchmarks** — tested Fran in your hotel gym on holiday? Log it yourself. Coach also enters benchmarks tested in class — both paths feed the same progress chart
- **Lifts** — hit a new 1RM at a friend's gym? Add it from the Lifts page. Coach-entered rep-max tests from Score Entry auto-sync to your lift records too, so PRs from class are captured without you lifting a finger
- **Personal activity log** — alongside the Forge logbook, log your own non-class workouts on a Personal toggle: swims, runs, hikes, holiday gym sessions, external CrossFit drop-ins, anything. Quick-add a date, activity type, duration (min), distance (km), effort (1-5), and notes. Pick from a curated preset list (Schwimmen, Laufen, Radfahren, Yoga, Wandern, Klettern, Inlinern, Gehen, Externes CrossFit, Anderes Studio) — or pick "Sonstiges" and type your own name (Tennis, Ski, anything you do). Your custom names save to your personal dropdown for next time
- **Use the app as your full training tracker** — not just for class days

### Track Your Benchmarks

- **16 standard CrossFit benchmarks** — Fran, Grace, Murph, Filthy Fifty, and more. Every time your coach programs one in class, your result is logged automatically. You can also enter results yourself for benchmarks tested outside class (holiday gyms, home workouts, anywhere)
- **Forge benchmarks** — your gym's own signature workouts, tracked the same way as the classics. These are the workouts that define your community
- **Progress charts** — watch your benchmark times improve over months and years on a clean visual chart
- **Scaling levels recorded** — RX vs Scaled is captured automatically (by the coach in class) or by you (for outside-class entries) so your progress chart tells the full story

### Track Your Lifts

- **All major barbell movements** — Back Squat, Front Squat, Deadlift, Bench Press, Overhead Press, Snatch, Clean & Jerk, and more
- **Multiple rep schemes** — 1RM, 3RM, 5RM, and 10RM tracked separately for each lift
- **Coach-entered records sync automatically** — when your coach programs a rep-max test (1RM, 3RM, 5RM, or 10RM) in class and enters your weight in Score Entry, your lift records, progress charts, and calculated 1RM all update instantly. Most of your PRs land in the app without you doing anything
- **You can also log lifts yourself** — for anything tested outside class (gym sessions, friend's garage, holiday) the Lifts page lets you add a record manually with weight + reps. Mobile-friendly stepper buttons with hold-to-repeat make it quick on the phone
- **Built-in 1RM calculator** — enter any weight and rep count, and the app estimates your one-rep max. Useful when you hit a heavy set of 3 and want to know where your 1RM sits
- **PR detection** — every new personal record is automatically highlighted, regardless of who entered the score

### Your Personal Records

- **Records gallery** — all your PRs across benchmarks and lifts, displayed in one place. Collapse and expand groups for quick navigation
- **Shareable cards** — generate a branded image of your PR with your photo, the workout details, and the gym logo. Perfect for posting to Instagram, WhatsApp, or your gym's group chat
- **Track your journey** — see at a glance how much you've improved since you started

### Community & Competition

- **Workout leaderboards** — after each WOD, see how your result compares to other athletes at the gym. Ranked by time, reps, or weight depending on the workout
- **See what you're scoring** — tapping a section chip shows the full workout description so you always know exactly which set of movements is being ranked
- **Fair ranking** — scaling levels are separated, tracks (1/2/3) rank above scaling, ties are broken properly, and the leaderboard reflects genuine performance
- **Smart grouping** — same-named workouts within 60 days are grouped so you see each athlete's best result across sessions
- **Gender filtering** — toggle between All, Male, and Female views with re-ranked positions
- **Fist bumps** — tap to give encouragement on any athlete's logged result. A simple way to recognise effort, celebrate PRs, and build community. Tap once to give, tap again to see who reacted, long-press to remove

### Achievements

- **Earn badges** — your coach defines achievements across categories like Strength, Gymnastics, Endurance, and Consistency
- **Tier and difficulty** — achievements come in Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum levels. Tier tracks your progression within a branch; difficulty reflects how hard the achievement is overall
- **Bodyweight calculator built in** — achievements like "Bench Press @ 50% Bodyweight" automatically display the target weight in kg based on your profile weight. No mental math at the bar
- **Difficulty filtering** — filter your achievement list to a specific level with one tap
- **Claim your own** — hit a milestone? Claim the achievement yourself and your coach will verify it
- **Build your collection** — your earned badges appear on your profile

### Workout Timer

- **6 built-in timer modes** — For Time, AMRAP, EMOM, Tabata, Intervals, and Hold
- **Voice cues** — audible round / interval announcements so you don't need to keep glancing at your phone
- **Fullscreen mode** — prop your phone up and use it as your personal timer during a workout
- **Custom Intervals** — build any multi-round work/rest configuration. Quick Fill a standard warm-up like "12 rounds of 50s work / 10s rest" in two taps, or build descending-work sessions (Rd 1: 50/10, Rd 2: 40/20, Rd 3: 30/30…) with per-round control. Add, duplicate, or delete rounds; live total-duration display
- **Named routines that follow you across devices** — save any Intervals configuration with a custom name (e.g. "Warm-up 12×50/10") and reload it on any device you're signed in on
- **No extra app needed** — the timer is built right in, ready when you need it

### Notifications

- **Stay in the loop** — receive push notifications when:
  - A new workout is published
  - You set a new personal record
  - Someone fist-bumps your result
  - You earn an achievement
  - You're promoted from the waitlist into a class
  - Your subscription is approaching its renewal date
- **You're in control** — choose exactly which notifications you want. Turn off what you don't need

### Family Accounts

- **One login for the whole family** — if your spouse or children also train at The Forge, they're linked under your account
- **Separate profiles** — each family member has their own logbook, benchmarks, lifts, and records
- **Easy switching** — toggle between profiles with a tap at the top of the screen
- **Book for your kids** — parents can manage class bookings for the whole family from one account
- **Family-shared 10-Card** — a single 10-card on the parent's account can cover bookings for the whole family. Every kid's session debits the parent's card automatically, with the same refund rules. Each child can also have their own card if preferred

## Class Booking

Even without a paid subscription, all members can:

- **View the weekly schedule** — see every class with its time, type, and available spots
- **Book your spot** — reserve your place in a class, or join the waitlist if it's full
- **Booking-window countdown** — every class card shows how long is left to book — "Closes in 1d 4h" / "3h 12m" / "14m". Turns amber under 2 hours and red under 30 minutes so you don't miss the cutoff
- **Cancel easily** — plans change. Cancel your booking with a tap. If you're on a 10-card and cancel inside the refund window, your session goes back on the card automatically
- **Book for family** — reserve spots for your spouse or children at the same time
- **See your 10-card balance** — if you train on a drop-in pass, the app shows your sessions remaining and card expiry date
- **Get pulled in from the waitlist** — if a confirmed athlete cancels or no-shows, you can be promoted into the class. A push notification tells you you're in

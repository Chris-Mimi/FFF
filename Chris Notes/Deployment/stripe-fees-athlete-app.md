# Stripe Fees on Athlete App Subscriptions

Standard German Stripe pricing (as of 2026). If you've negotiated custom rates with Stripe, your numbers may differ. Check **Stripe Dashboard → Reports → Balance** for actuals.

All figures include the **+0.5% Billing surcharge** on recurring transactions.

---

## Member tier (€8/mo · €80/yr)

### Per-charge fees on €8 monthly

| Payment method | Fee per €8 charge | Fee × 12 months | Net for the year |
|---|---|---|---|
| EEA card | ~€0.41 (1.5% + €0.25 + 0.5% billing) | **~€4.92** | ~€91.08 |
| Non-EEA card | ~€0.55 (3.25% + €0.25 + 0.5% billing) | ~€6.60 | ~€89.40 |
| SEPA Direct Debit | ~€0.35 (0.8% + €0.25 + 0.5% billing) | ~€4.20 | ~€91.80 |

### Monthly vs yearly comparison (Member tier)

| Plan | Gross revenue | Stripe fees (EEA card) | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12× €8 monthly | €96.00 | ~€4.92 | **~€91.08** |
| €80 yearly | €80.00 | ~€1.65 | **~€78.35** |

**Net difference: monthly nets you €12.73 more per athlete.**

## Wellpass / Standard tier (€10/mo · €100/yr)

Applies to Wellpass, 10-card, Hansefit, and Drop-in athletes.

### Per-charge fees on €10 monthly

| Payment method | Fee per €10 charge | Fee × 12 months | Net for the year |
|---|---|---|---|
| EEA card | ~€0.45 (1.5% + €0.25 + 0.5% billing) | **~€5.40** | ~€114.60 |
| Non-EEA card | ~€0.63 (3.25% + €0.25 + 0.5% billing) | ~€7.56 | ~€112.44 |
| SEPA Direct Debit | ~€0.38 (0.8% + €0.25 + 0.5% billing) | ~€4.56 | ~€115.44 |

### Monthly vs yearly comparison (Wellpass tier)

| Plan | Gross revenue | Stripe fees (EEA card) | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12× €10 monthly | €120.00 | ~€5.40 | **~€114.60** |
| €100 yearly | €100.00 | ~€2.25 | **~€97.75** |

**Net difference: monthly nets you €16.85 more per athlete.**

---

## Why monthly wins on net revenue

The fixed **€0.25 per-transaction fee** is a much larger % of an €8/€10 charge than of a €100 charge. So yearly plans are more *fee-efficient* (lower fee %) — but the lower yearly price wipes out that efficiency and then some.

**Takeaway:** yearly plans are a customer convenience / loyalty discount, not a revenue optimisation.

---

## Caveats

- German VAT (19%) applies to Stripe fees if you haven't registered your VAT ID with Stripe.
- Amex / corporate cards typically incur a higher surcharge (~3% or more).
- These are published rates only; large merchants negotiate lower rates.
